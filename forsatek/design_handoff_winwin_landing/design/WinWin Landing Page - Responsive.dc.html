The problem

Zaynab. London. Powered wheelchair.

She's never been to Dubai. She's tried to plan it three times.

She travels around Europe a few times a year with her sister. She has never been to Dubai, but she would love to go. Travelling so far away is frightening, because the information she needs simply is not there.

She started planning a trip a few times, but never finished.

?What kind of ramp does the hotel have?

The whole journey

Is the whole journey accessible?

It is a full-time job for Zaynab to research all Dubai communities and call hundreds of hotels to find accommodation that fits her needs.

Flights
Mostly findable

Emirates does this really well — accessibility information for flights is easy to find. Most other airlines do not.

Hotels
Almost invisible

Ramp slopes, door widths, roll-in showers, turning space — rarely published, rarely verified.

City & neighbourhood
Fragmented

Information about Dubai itself is scattered, so pulling it together takes real effort.

Attractions & experiences
Almost invisible

What she can actually do once she is there is the hardest thing of all to find out.

What we do

Every step of her journey, verified.

To help Zaynab plan her trip we have to be sure every step is accessible. That is why we focus on two pillars.

First pillar

We ask every hotel more than 120 questions

We contact hotels directly to get in-depth, measured data on their accessibility — not marketing claims.

Second pillar

We collect the city, not just the hotel

From public sources, from Dubai, from partner travel companies, and from local communities.

As a result, we have everything Zaynab was missing to go to Dubai.
